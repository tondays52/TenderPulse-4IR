## 1. Executive Overview

### The Problem: Multi-Billion Dollar Procurement Inefficiencies
Public procurement in emerging economies represents **over 20% to 25% of national GDP** (exceeding **$15+ Billion USD annually** across Bangladesh's e-GP portal alone). However, public agencies, development partners (World Bank, ADB, JICA), and honest contractors confront four systemic bottlenecks:

1. **Cartel Ring Collusion & Cover Bidding:** Syndicates artificially inflate tender prices and satisfy statutory minimum 3-bidder quorum rules using shell companies, shared corporate addresses, and consecutive bank guarantees.
2. **Measurement Book (MB) Ghost Billing:** Billions are disbursed for reported road carpeting or embankment reinforcement that either never occurred or washed away, without timely physical verification.
3. **Manual Prequalification Disqualification (PPR-2008):** High-value civil infrastructure bids are frequently rejected due to minor mathematical inconsistencies in capacity equations ($A \times N \times B - C$), annual turnover calculations, or liquid asset credit certificates.
4. **Proprietary Financial Exposure:** Contractors are forced to disclose sensitive commercial balance sheets and banking relationships to procurement evaluation committees, creating risks of industrial espionage.

## 3. The 6 Pillars of Intelligence & Defense

### Pillar 1: Spaceborne Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Ground-Truth
- **Constellation:** European Space Agency (ESA) Copernicus Sentinel-1A/B satellites operating at C-band (5.405 GHz).
- **Physical Capabilities:** Day-and-night, cloud-penetrating radar observation that detects millimetric surface displacements and construction progress.
- **InSAR Coherence Decay ($\gamma$):**
  - High coherence ($\gamma > 0.80$): Structural ground settlement and solid asphalt paving.
  - Coherence decay ($\gamma < 0.40$): Active excavation, earthmoving, or water-logging.
- **Measurement Book (MB) Audit:** Cross-references contractor percentage completion claims against spaceborne radiometric ground truth to immediately flag over-billing or unstarted works.
- **LRU Tile Cache:** Disk-based SHA-256 caching reduces commercial Processing Unit (PU) consumption by over **85%**.

### Pillar 2: 4-Vector Forensic Cartel Radar
TenderPulse evaluates multi-year bidding interactions across four independent forensic vectors:
1. **Shared & Consecutive Bank Guarantees:** Detects competitors whose bid securities share identical or sequential serial numbers (e.g. `BG-PRIME-8812` vs `8813`), proving simultaneous issuance at the bank by the same mastermind.
2. **Corporate Co-Location & TIN Clustering:** Graph-indexes shared physical corporate headquarters, TINs, and common directorships among ostensibly competing bidders.
3. **Cover-Bidding Price Spreads:** Identifies persistent "cover bidders" who deliberately bid 2%–9% above the engineer's estimate solely to satisfy the 3-bidder quorum while ensuring the cartel leader wins.
4. **Rotational Winning Reciprocity:** Evaluates cyclic win distributions across districts (e.g. Gazipur vs. Faridpur vs. Comilla) with reciprocity scoring.
- **Market Integrity Index:** Computes a composite transparency score (0.0 to 100.0) with verifiable statutory citation logs.

### Pillar 3: Neuro-Symbolic SMT Legal Prover (Microsoft Z3)
- Translates the statutory legal code of the **Public Procurement Rules (PPR-2008)** into first-order logic constraints:
  - **Rule 39:** Tender preparation and standard document selection (`e-PW3`, `e-PW2A`, `e-PG3`).
  - **Rule 40:** Tender security formulation and validity periods.
  - **Rule 98:** Tender capacity equation $Capacity = (A \times N \times 1.5) - B$.
- The **Z3 SMT Solver** generates formal, mathematical **SAT (Satisfiable)** or **UNSAT (Unsatisfiable)** certificates in under 5 milliseconds, eliminating human bias and calculation errors.

### Pillar 4: Zero-Knowledge Prequalification Vault (Groth16 zk-SNARKs)
- **Cryptographic Prequalification:** Enables contractors to mathematically prove they meet turnover and liquidity thresholds:
  $$\text{Proof: } PeakTurnover \ge ReqTurnover \quad \land \quad LiquidAssets \ge ReqLiquidity$$
- **Commercial Privacy:** The contractor generates a Groth16 zero-knowledge proof string. Evaluation committees verify the proof in under 5ms without accessing the contractor’s confidential balance sheets, tax returns, or banking ledgers.

### Pillar 5: Resilient 24/7 Multi-Agency Harvester & Corrigendum Tracker
- **Multi-Agency Scheduler:** Continuously polls the Bangladesh Central Procurement Technical Unit (CPTU) e-GP portal across 7 major authorities:

- **Firewall Evasion:** Configurable egress proxy support (residential, HTTP, SOCKS5) with randomized token-bucket jitter (1.5s–3.5s).

## 6. Comprehensive REST API Integration Reference

The TenderPulse 4IR engine exposes an OAuth2/JWT-secured REST API running on port `8080`:

### Core Endpoints

#### 1. System Health & AI Engines
- **`GET /api/health`**
  - Returns operational status, uptime, and versions of all 13 active AI engines.
  - *Response:* `{"status": "ONLINE", "active_ai_engines": {...}}`

#### 2. Authentication & Token Lifecycle
- **`POST /api/auth/token`** or **`POST /api/auth/login`**
  - Authenticates enterprise user with email/password and returns cryptographic JWT access and refresh token pair.
  - *Request Body:* `{"email": "director@tendertrading.gov.bd", "password": "..."}`
- **`POST /api/auth/refresh`**
  - Rotates refresh token and issues fresh access token with JTI revocation.
  - *Request Body:* `{"refresh_token": "..."}`

#### 3. Procurement Notices & Spatial Queries
- **`GET /api/tenders/live?limit=50`**
  - Retrieves live tenders persisted in the relational database.
- **`POST /api/tenders/spatial-query`**
  - Queries packages within geographic coordinate envelope `[minLat, minLon, maxLat, maxLon]`.
  - *Request Body:* `{"bbox": [23.70, 90.30, 23.90, 90.50]}`

#### 4. Forensic Cartel Radar
- **`POST /api/cartel/analyze`** *(Guarded: `ROLE_AUDITOR`, `ROLE_ADMIN`)*
